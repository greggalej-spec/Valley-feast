Our kitchen

Seasoned by the Northern Range

Valley Feast started the way all good Trini food starts — one big pot, plenty family, and neighbours who kept coming back with containers. Today we cook breakfast and lunch for the valley every morning, and cater feasts across Trinidad, from office limes to wedding receptions.

“If the pot doesn’t taste like your grandmother watching over your shoulder, it doesn’t leave the kitchen.”
— Greg Leotaud & Amanda Laban, the Valley Feast family
