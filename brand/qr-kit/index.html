Before you print

Keep it scanning

Print it big enough

Keep the code itself at least 25 mm wide. The label prints it at 30 mm and the counter card at 50 mm. Never shrink a lockup below its listed size.

Leave the cream border alone

Scanners need the empty cream space around the code. Don't trim into it, print over it, or butt it against a dark edge.

Keep it flat

On a bottle, place the label on the flat face, away from the seam and the curve of the shoulder. Matte stock scans better than glossy under shop lights.

One-colour jobs

For stamps or a single-ink sticker run, use the code-mono files: black on white, same design.
